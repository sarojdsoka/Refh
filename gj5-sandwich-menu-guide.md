## Signature Sauce Blends (Make These)

### 1. Tandoori Mayo
`Tandoori sauce + (mayo if available) + pinch of chaat masala`
Use as middle-layer spread

### 2. Peri Peri Cheese Sauce
`Peri peri sauce + Liquid cheese (50:50)`
Drizzle on top after grilling

### 3. Makhni Chipotle
`Makhni sauce + Chipotle dip (3:1)`
Rich, smoky, creamy

### 4. Harissa Thousand Island
`Thousand Island + Harissa (3:1)`
Spicy-tangy twist

### 5. Schezwan Cheese Blast
`Schezwan sauce + Liquid cheese (1:2)`
For cheese burst effect

### 6. Jalapeno Pizza Base
`Pizza pasta sauce + Jalapeno dip (2:1)`
Base layer sauce with kick

## Assembly Method
1. Take bread slices, apply butter on outer sides
2. Spread sauces on inner sides as per layer chart
3. Add filling vegetables
4. Add cheese (grated + slice + liquid cheese)
5. Close sandwich, grill until golden brown
6. Cut diagonally, drizzle liquid cheese / sauce on top
7. Serve with suggested dip

## Pro Tips
- **Liquid cheese drizzle** after grilling = Instagram-worthy cheese pull
- Serve with **matching complimentary dip**
- Pre-mix sauce blends in batches for consistency
- Add **sev / crushed chips** inside for crunch
- **Chaat masala + oregano** sprinkle on top after grilling
- **Toast bread with butter + garlic powder** for extra flavor
- **Harissa + Liquid Cheese** drizzle = unique USP (rare in market)
- **Tandoori + Chipotle** combo = smoky-sweet balance customers remember
